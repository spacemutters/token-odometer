How far have you driven Claude?

Pick your Claude Code history folder and every token in it rolls onto the odometer. It's counted on this computer. This page can't send anything anywhere.

Where is the folder?
Windows
C:\Users\you\.claude\projects
Mac
~/.claude/projects. In the picker, press ⌘ ⇧ . to show hidden folders.
Linux
~/.claude/projects

Claude Code clears history older than 30 days unless you've changed cleanupPeriodDays in its settings, so the odometer counts what's still on your disk.
